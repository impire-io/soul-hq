precisely: "what we just did was apply the looks, not the feel. The
goal of the design was to be calm." The diagnosis held — the canon
pass had moved tokens and chrome while the screens kept the first
canon's density under new paint — and the answer was a
screen-by-screen walk with the operator, each screen shown live off
the rig before moving on. The calm devices, named from the kit and
applied: one thing leads per screen (the act key rides the page

the earned normal, the exceptions speak, and the record's own screen

system-status screen); creation waits in the slide-over; lowercase

paper — whose still rides alignment, the mention hardens to an ink
shoulder, Reply waits for pointer or keyboard. The central measure:
**zero act endpoints changed, and the calm pass's first commit
touched no module render file's behavior — it took things away**

declare limits was refused by design 0009's own test in its own words

[mechanism-argument, the test as the design's tripwire]. And one dead

the conversations there were, so a fresh board walled the wizard in.
