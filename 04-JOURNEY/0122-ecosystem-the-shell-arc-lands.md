# Episode 0122 — The shell arc lands: both human ends on the spine (2026-08-21)

The last carve-out of episodes 0118/0119 closes: the shell halves both
graduated designs named as their [O]s are designed (shell 0005/0006),
built, and walked end to end. With them, episode 0116's three asks —
the storage explorer, external tools, the guardrail's human end — are
all screens a person can reach.

**The plumbing first (soulstream v0.13.0-rc.10)**: the persona scope
grows the `grants.>` and `approvals.>` tails — the deployment duty
grants.md D30 and approvals.md D43 always stated, paid once for both
ceremonies under the shared-source rule (SC-004). Without it, a person
in the house could not link a grant or ask after their own tickets;
found scoping the arc, closed before it.

**The tools module (shell design
[`0005`](../02-DESIGN/soulstream-shell/0005-the-tools-module.md))**:
one screen where the catalog's tools stand with each person's own
standing joined on. Connecting is the person's own ceremony — out to
the provider's sign-in, back through the module's callback (OAuth
`state` carrying the broker's link id, its published contract),
completed only on the session that started it because the plane holds
the ceremony under their persona. The admin's add writes **both halves
in one act** (plane first, catalog second, each failure named); removal
reverses both and says standing custody survives. The secret crosses
once — never echoed, never in the catalog, sealed at rest.

**The approvals module (shell design
[`0006`](../02-DESIGN/soulstream-shell/0006-the-approvals-module.md))**:
the guardrail's deferred acts as a screen — who asked, what, under
which rule, how long the window has left, **never the arguments** (the
ticket's privacy line, said out loud so the absence reads as designed).
One tap **mints the person's own signed answer and delivers it**: the
yes is the person's (their key, their signature, verified by the plane
against the directory and the rule's approvers clause), the delivery is
the surface's (the originator is an agent that is not in this browser,
so the signed artifact is carried to its tail on the node-standing
lane). Mint and delivery kept apart in the design's own words —
delegated authority, never borrowed identity, with delivery named as
the third thing it is. Activation is the deployment's declared
`GuardrailOn` fact, the AdminBase/AgentsDial pattern's third rider.

**Measured** (the consumer-position gate, against soulnode
v0.13.0-rc.10 published): the whole tools ceremony — admin add from
the screen, the authorize redirect out with PKCE and the callback
back, the standing showing connected, the custody scan over the
deployment's disk finding the secret nowhere with the control fired,
disconnect, both-halves removal — and the whole approvals loop
**through a browser-shaped session for the first time**: a rule loaded
live naming the signed-in person its approver, a real op deferred, the
ticket on the screen with its count on the spine, the tap, the
originator's retry served, the ticket reading spent, the deny arm
ending denied. Plus the two modules joining the
no-module-imports-another walk and every standing gate green on the
new pins.

Shipped as **shell v0.11.0-rc.2** (with the e2e's plane-rename
catch-up riding along) and **soulstream v0.13.0-rc.11** — the house
composing the shell that has both ends and declaring the guardrail
fact it already made true in rc.9. The candidate the operator soaks
now carries the entire 0116 evaluation, closed: a person can add a
tool, connect their account, watch their agent use it as them, and
answer the guardrail from the spine.

What it taught: the rig composes the shell itself, so a declared fact
can reach the shell's activation from the gate before the product
ships its pass-through — the 0101 two-step (shell first, product
follow-up) collapsed into one arc without weakening the
declared-never-probed rule.

Reversal condition: the designs' own stand ([`0005`] §5's per-render
linked-status reads, [`0006`] §6's per-render pending counts — both
reopen as support-layer caching if real deployments chafe; the
argument-visibility line reopens as richer *rule* vocabulary, never as
arguments on tickets). For the arc itself: none — records completed
builds and measurements.
